==================== SHORT DESCRIPTION ====================
Feed your fires by hand. Put or throw fuel into a campfire, stove, fireplace or forge and it burns — no menu. Stove doors open as you bring wood, and a fire can feed itself from the woodpile next to it or from a firewood bin.

==================== LONG DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ====================
[center][size=6][b]Immersive Fire[/b][/size]
[size=3][i]Feed the fire by hand — no menu, no progress bar.[/i][/size][/center]

In The Long Dark, the fire is the heart of every night. You split the wood, carry it in, stack it by the stove — and then you open a menu, pick a stick from a list and watch a bar fill up.

[b]Immersive Fire lets you do it the way it looks.[/b] Put a log into the stove and it burns. Toss a stick at the campfire and the fire takes it. Stack your firewood next to the stove, and it keeps itself going while you sleep.

[line]
[size=5][b]Features at a glance[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Put fuel in — it burns[/b]: campfires, fireplaces, stoves, barrels, the forge and the ammo workbench.
[*][b]Fires draw fuel in[/b]: carry or throw fuel near the fire and it flies in.
[*][b]Stove doors[/b] open by themselves when you bring fuel, and close after.
[*][b]Auto-feed[/b]: a fire adds fuel from the woodpile next to it when it burns low.
[*][b]Firewood bin[/b]: a craftable bin a fire can feed from.
[*][b]The game's rules stay[/b]: wind, burn limits and coal rules work as usual.
[/list]

[line]
[size=5][b]Put fuel in — it burns[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Campfires[/b] — fuel put inside the stone ring of a burning campfire burns, as if you added it from the menu.
[*][b]Fireplaces and open hearths[/b] — fuel put into a burning fireplace burns.
[*][b]Stoves and barrels[/b] — fuel put to the fire burns. Items on the stove top and on cooking spots are left alone.
[*][b]Forge[/b] — fuel put into a burning forge burns, following the forge's rules.
[*][b]Ammo workbench[/b] — its furnace takes fuel just like a stove.
[/list]

[size=5][b]The fire draws fuel in[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Carry or throw fuel near a burning campfire, and the fire draws it in.
[*]In front of a stove door there is a "funnel": fuel that lands there flies in through the door. Fuel put at the sides of the stove goes in too.
[*]Stove doors open by themselves when you bring fuel close, and close again once it is in.
[*]Groups of items carried with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/thelongdark/mods/125]Perfect Placement[/url] 0.32.0+ burn together.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Auto-feed[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Hold [b]Left Shift[/b] (configurable) in the fire menu and choose when the fire adds fuel by itself: when less than [b]15, 30 or 60 minutes[/b] of burning are left.
[*]The fire takes the fuel lying in its zones — the woodpile next to the stove is all it needs.
[*]The [b]Auto-feed[/b] button can take the place of "Add fuel" in the fire menu (stoves, barrels and forges; optional for campfires).
[*]Your auto-feed choices are kept in your save.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Firewood bin[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]A large wooden bin with a hinged lid, crafted from [b]6 softwood[/b].
[*]Open and close the lid; a fire nearby with auto-feed on takes its fuel from the bin.
[/list]

[size=5][b]The game's rules stay[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]When the fire can't take the fuel (wind, the fire already burns long enough, too early for coal), you see the game's own message. The fuel stays where it lies and burns once it can.
[*][b]Unread skill books[/b]: ask (like the game), never burn, or burn — your choice.
[*]With [b]More Fuels & Accelerants[/b], tinder goes into the fire when its "tinder as fuel" setting is on.
[*]Near a fire, the item you carry can go without the green or red highlight, so putting fuel in looks natural.
[/list]

[line]
[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]
Everything is in [b]Mod Settings → Immersive Fire[/b]. Every option has a description in English and Russian.
[spoiler]Show all settings
[list]
[*][b]Fires[/b] — campfires, fireplaces, stoves and barrels, forge: each can be turned off.
[*][b]Zones[/b] — how big the campfire and stove zones are, and the funnel in front of a stove door.
[*][b]Rules[/b] — unread skill books, and the game's messages when a fire can't take fuel.
[*][b]Auto-feed[/b] — mode (pull and auto-feed, pull only, auto-feed only), the button instead of "Add fuel", the key for the time choice.
[*][b]Animation and look[/b] — how fast fuel flies in, stove door timing, the item highlight near a fire.
[/list]
[/spoiler]

[size=5][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Fuel lies in the fire but doesn't burn.[/b]
The fire has to be burning (not just lit), and the game must allow more fuel: no strong wind, not over the burn limit, coal only into a hot fire. With Messages on, you see why.

[b]Does it work with modded fuel?[/b]
Yes — whatever the game counts as fuel. Tinder burns only with More Fuels & Accelerants and its "tinder as fuel" setting.

[b]My stove takes fuel from too far away / not far enough.[/b]
Change the zone and funnel sizes in the Zones section.
