==================== SHORT DESCRIPTION ====================
Cook with what lies next to the fire. Pots, food, water and recipe ingredients lying by the fire show up in the cooking menu and fly into the pot one by one — no more emptying your pack onto the floor and picking it back up.

==================== LONG DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ====================
[center][size=6][b]Immersive Cooking[/b][/size]
[size=3][i]Cook with what lies next to the fire.[/i][/size][/center]

In The Long Dark, a camp kitchen is a real place. The pot sits by the stove, the meat lies on the table, the water bottles stand in a row. And yet the game only sees your backpack: to cook, you pick everything up, cook, and put it all back down.

[b]Immersive Cooking lets the fire see what is around it.[/b] The pot by the stove goes onto the fire. The meat on the table shows up in the cooking menu. Ingredients fly into the pot one by one — and your pack stays as it was.

[line]
[size=5][b]Features at a glance[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Cookware nearby[/b] — pots and pans lying by the fire can be used, and move onto it by themselves.
[*][b]Food and water nearby[/b] — the cooking menu, recipes and boiling use what lies by the fire.
[*][b]Flying ingredients[/b] — what is taken flies into the pot one at a time.
[*][b]Skip recipe preparation[/b] — the whole recipe takes exactly the time the menu shows.
[*]Works at every fire: campfires, stoves, barrels and fireplaces.
[/list]

[line]
[size=5][b]Cookware and food next to the fire[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Cookware[/b] — empty pots and pans lying near a fire show up when you choose cookware for Cook or Water. The chosen one moves onto the fire by itself (the same item — nothing is duplicated). Cookware you carry is offered first.
[*][b]Cook button[/b] — the fire menu gets a Cook action that puts nearby cookware on the fire and opens the cooking menu.
[*][b]Food[/b] — the cooking menu lists food lying near the fire, in the same order the game uses.
[*][b]Recipes[/b] — ingredients, cooking oil, broth, flour and drinking water lying near the fire count. Exactly the needed amount is taken; what you carry is used first.
[*][b]Boiling water[/b] — unsafe water lying near the fire can be boiled.
[*][b]Place[/b] — loose food lying near the fire can be put on it with Place.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Flying ingredients[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]What the cooking, boiling or Place menu takes from around the fire flies into the cookware one at a time, around furniture.
[*]Food drops in over the rim; bottles, bags and broth come to the rim and pour; what is left goes back to its place.
[*]The game's sounds play as each one goes in, and the food shows in the pot once the last ingredient is in.
[*]Nothing changes in the game itself: items are used the moment you choose, so you can save at any time.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Small comforts[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Food stays usable[/b] — food put down next to a fire doesn't start warming up by itself (optional).
[*][b]Skip recipe preparation[/b] — a recipe goes straight into the pot; the dish cooks that much longer, so the whole recipe takes exactly the time shown. The skill point is still given (optional).
[*][b]Same food listed once[/b] — pieces of the same food and weight show as one line, so lots of meat doesn't push the recipes down (optional).
[*][b]Tidy fire menu[/b] — Place, Water and Cook show only when they can be used (optional).
[*][b]One switch[/b] — turn the whole mod off in its settings without removing it.
[/list]

[size=5][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Is anything duplicated or lost?[/b]
No. The pot that moves onto the fire is the same item, and recipes take exactly the needed amount.

[b]Can I save while ingredients are flying?[/b]
Yes. The flight is only for show: the items are used the moment you choose.

[b]How far from the fire does it look?[/b]
1.5 m by default; change the distance in the settings.
