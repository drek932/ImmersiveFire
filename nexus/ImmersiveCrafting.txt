==================== SHORT DESCRIPTION ====================
Tools and materials lying at a workbench count as if you carried them. Craft at a workbench, ammo workbench, forge or fire with what lies around it — no more emptying your pack and picking everything back up.

==================== LONG DESCRIPTION (BBCode) ====================
[center][size=6][b]Immersive Crafting[/b][/size]
[size=3][i]Your workbench, your tools.[/i][/size][/center]

In The Long Dark, a good base has a workshop. The hacksaw lies by the bench, the hides in a pile, the gunpowder waits on the ammo workbench. And yet the game only sees your backpack: to craft, you pick everything up, craft, and put it all back down.

[b]Immersive Crafting lets the workbench see what is on it.[/b] The tools and materials lying there count as if you carried them — and your pack stays as it was.

[line]
[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Tools nearby[/b] — tools lying next to a workbench, ammo workbench, furniture workbench, forge or a fire (crafting at a fire) can be selected for crafting.
[*][b]Materials nearby[/b] — items, weight-based materials, gunpowder and lamp oil lying there count and are used. Exactly the needed amount is taken. What you carry is used first, then what lies nearby (from the top down, the farthest first).
[*][b]Workbench drawer[/b] — tools and materials in small containers next to a workbench (up to 5 kg, like its own drawer) count too. Only unlocked, already searched containers; items lying openly are used first.
[*][b]Ice fishing holes[/b] — an axe, prybar or other tool lying next to a fishing hole can be used to cut or clear it.
[/list]

The crafting menu shows the real counts ("2 / 3"), including what lies nearby. Repairing and fishing itself are not changed.

[size=5][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Does it take items from my locked or unsearched containers?[/b]
No. Only unlocked containers you have already searched, up to 5 kg, next to a workbench.

[b]Which items are used first?[/b]
What you carry, then what lies openly nearby (from the top down, the farthest first), then the small containers.

[b]Will it count the same item twice with Immersive Cooking?[/b]
No. Recipes in the cooking menu belong to Immersive Cooking, all other crafting to Immersive Crafting.
